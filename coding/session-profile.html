Method and limits

Times are derived from persisted client-side event timestamps. Native v4 recordings are selected at their newest generation; inherited fork history is excluded using its tagged boundary. Unfinished work is reported as incomplete. Tokens are provider-reported and include cached input. This report supplies evidence for hypotheses; it does not establish a speedup or billed-cost reduction.
